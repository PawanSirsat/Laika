**Decisions**
- Enter always opens the day panel, even on a day with one task. LAI-1200
  opened a lone task directly. The panel holds *Move to…*, so the other way
  would leave a keyboard user no way to reschedule a single-task day.
- The day panel stays LAI-1178's per-cell `DayPanel`, not LAI-1200's floating
  `DayList`. The base split was to be kept, and the panel already carried
  *Move to…*.
- In Month view the period follows the focused day's month. Arrowing onto a
  padding day pages the month, as LAI-1200 did. A click on a padding day only
  moves the stop.
- PageUp/PageDown act only on the grid. The page-wide ← → paging LAI-1178 had
  is kept for when focus is elsewhere, and it now steps weeks in Week view.
  Making PageUp/PageDown page-wide too would take page scrolling away.
- `?week=` is the Monday, but any real date of the week is accepted. A date
  that does not exist (2026-02-31) falls back to this week rather than rolling
  over into the next month.
- LAI-1200's empty-period block came along. The restored test asks for it,
  and it matters more now: a month whose only work is overdue would otherwise
  show an empty grid. `nearestDueOutside` is fed only work that is not overdue.
