**Decisions**
- Series colour from the SpaceBadge's own generator, not `spaceColor()` /
  `--space-*` — the badge in the sidebar and in the Time table is
  `avatarColor('space:'+slug)`, so matching the badge means that hue. My first
  cut used `--space-*` and would have painted a third colour.
- Mix toward `--text-primary` rather than pick per-theme lightness — one
  string works in both themes (lighter in dark, deeper in light) and keeps
  `groupRows` pure and theme-free.
- Previous period from `days=2N`, not a new endpoint — the existing API only;
  90 days gets no comparison rather than a wrong one (180 > the server's 92).
- Comparison in secondary ink with ▲/▼ — more tokens is not good or bad.
- People keep the slot palette in the Time report (not avatar colours): the
  avatar generator's hues collide often across eight hues, and people are
  ordered A–Z, so slots stay distinct and stable within a report.
- LAI-1189 fixed here as the brief asked; its own file left in backlog for
  CHIEF to close (one task in progress per session).
