**Decisions**
- `status` is **nullable**, not NOT NULL as the plan's table reads: a person who sets only a working week must not show an *open* pill they never chose. Null is *not said*.
- The **space-admin** variant is decided by `project.members.manage` on a space (the lead's §3.2 power, implicit for owners and admins holding *Open every project*), not by `time.report.read_project`, which is LAI-1129's action and does not exist here. The hours column rides `people.analytics.read` alone for the same reason; T1 may widen both to its `time.report.*` actions.
- Org **viewers are left out of every variant**, not only the owner's: they cannot take work, and a list of who has room that names them is noise.
- `last_2_weeks_minutes` is **confirmed** minutes over the person's own last 14 days, one grouped read for the whole list. Drafts stay on the load read, which uses `summaryOf`.
- Logging the list writes one `analytics_views` row per listed person through `readLogged`, nested in the list's transaction (savepoints). D-135's ten-minute dedupe is LAI-1129's; until it lands, every list view writes a row per person.
- `POST /me/availability/away` answers `201` with the whole `AvailabilityView` rather than the one period, so the client redraws from one answer. Added two limits the plan did not name: 20 current and upcoming periods, 366 days each.
- The load's `projects` rows carry `hidden`: spaces the reader cannot open fold into one `hidden: true` row, kept apart from time on no space.
