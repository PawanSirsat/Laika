**Decisions**
- **Scoped it to the frame and said so twice.** LAI-285 already made the drawer
  two columns at 1120px, which is the shape a Jira modal has; what the owner is
  asking for is the *box*. Named the out-of-scope half explicitly — breadcrumb,

- **Centred in the scrimmed region, not the viewport.** The scrim starts at
  `--rail-width` so the sidebar stays usable with a task open — LAI-252 did that
  deliberately. Dimming the whole window to gain symmetry trades a working
  behaviour for a few pixels. Recorded the alternative in D-063 as a revisit
  trigger rather than leaving it unsaid.
- **Folded the dialog semantics into the same task.** `TaskDrawer.tsx` has no
  `role`, no `aria-modal`, no label and no focus trap today — it focuses the
  panel and listens for Escape, which is most of the behaviour and none of the
  semantics. **A panel pinned to an edge is arguably a complementary region; a
  centred box over a dimmed page is a modal dialog and nothing else.** So this is
  not scope creep onto the owner's request — it is what the request makes true.
  Said that in D-063 in those words so it does not read as me padding the task.
- **Required a maximum height, asserted at two viewport heights.** A drawer is
  viewport-height by construction and a centred box is not; "as tall as its
  content" is how a modal ends up with its close button off-screen. Same
  two-height reasoning as LAI-475, for the same reason: one height is what a
  wrong constant already satisfies.
- **Renaming `drawer/` is allowed but not required, and in its own commit.** The
  name is wrong after this, but a rename tangled into the reshaping diff makes
  the reshaping unreviewable.
