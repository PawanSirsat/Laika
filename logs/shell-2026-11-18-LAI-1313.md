**Decisions**
- The strip's *today* is My day's (`view.tz`, by `joined_at`), not the Meetings page's browser-local day by `scheduled_start` — the entry it makes lands on the day the page names; a meeting joined after midnight counts on the day it was attended.
- *Sync now* reads `last_run` from the answer, not the HTTP status: the server records a Graph refusal and still answers 200, so a status-only toast would say *synced* over a refused grant.
- A failed strip read draws nothing rather than an error card above the Timeline — the strip is a shortcut onto what the Meetings page already offers.
- Meeting review asks titles with a ref of asked ids, not an effect keyed on the title map: keyed on the map, each answer re-ran the effect and aborted the others in flight.
- No server change was needed: `source` (`teams:<id>`) is already on the client type; the title comes from the existing participant-only `GET /meetings/:id`.
