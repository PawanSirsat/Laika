**Decisions.**
- In *Everything in review* a decidable row keeps its checkbox, so the bar
  works in both views; only the rows the reader may not decide are read-only
  (no box, no Accept / Send back, *Open on board* and why). The plan's
  "read-only toggle" is read as being about those rows; said in the task file.
- The tiles count the open view *before* the filters. The filters narrow the
  list; the tiles say what the view holds. The existing cue test's
  `[data-stat="check"]` assertion relied on exactly that.
- The view toggle writes the address with `replace`, as a filter does (the
  `use-route.ts` rule), not `push`.
- The bar is `position: sticky; bottom` inside `.rev`, not the List's
  `absolute` bar: the queue is sections down a page, not one scrolling card,
  and the List's bar measures `.list-card` to make room for itself.
- `can_decide` is read as the server sends it; no client-side guess from
  memberships. `decidableCount` lives in `api/review.ts` because `api/` may
  not import `routes/`.
- The bulk run keeps the refused ids selected and clears the accepted ones
  (a `new Set(outcome.failed…)`), so after the re-read a retry is one click.
