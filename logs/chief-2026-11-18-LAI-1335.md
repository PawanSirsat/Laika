**Decisions**
- "It joins the regular run (no overlap)" is read as *one flight*: a backfill never joins a
  regular run (that would answer three days for N), it waits for it and then runs; a regular
  tick or *Sync now* during a backfill joins the backfill, whose window holds theirs. A
  backfill behind another backfill waits too, rather than answer someone else's day count.
- Reads stay whole before any write, as the regular run's, so a failed read still writes
  nothing; only the writes are paced. A switch turned off between two people stops the rest
  and keeps who was written (each person rests on their own complete read).
- The backfill records its counts as the connection's last sync, as any run does, so a page
  that loses the answer (a proxy timing out a long request) still sees them; the confirm says
  so.
- An answer Graph adds later is not logged until decided: the set is explicit.
