**Decisions**
- A new read rather than widening `/me/time/summary`. The summary's shape is
  pinned by Overview and the Time report, and Month/Year need a per-day state
  and tasks that the summary has no place for.
- The cap is the summary's own `stretch()` (92 days), so a year is four reads
  on the client. That is cheaper than inventing a second bound.
- `tz` is optional, defaulting to the drafts' zone as `GET /me/time` does. It
  only decides `today`, and so `future` versus `no_signal`.
