**Decisions**
- Category/status agreement is a composite FK, not service discipline — a raw `UPDATE tasks SET status` now fails in SQL; 13 test fixtures did exactly that and had to change, which is the point.
- FK `NO ACTION`, not `RESTRICT`: project delete cascades into both tables, and RESTRICT checks before the other cascade lands.
- Copy in SQL, not a boot backfill: the contract wanted copy-and-drop in one migration, so ids are ULIDs built from `random() & 31` (not `abs(random())`, which overflows on INT64_MIN).
- A bare category the task is already in is still `409`, not a hop to that category's first status — otherwise a webhook or agent saying `in_progress` would undo a person's column choice.
- Every status write answers with the whole list; `POST` without position lands after the last status of its category.
- `PATCH /tasks/:id` still refuses `status`/`status_id` (§6.4, LAI-130), though the contract listed PATCH; `/status` is the one status write.
- Reused `task.status_changed` (ids and names in the payload) and `project.updated` — no new verb, so no third owner.
