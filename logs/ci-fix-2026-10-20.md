**Decisions**
- Diagnosed the header before changing it. The word `EFFORT` is in the
  self-hosted Plus Jakarta Sans, so it is the same everywhere. The resting `↕`
  (U+2195) is outside the shipped fonts' unicode ranges (latin has U+2191 and
  U+2193 only), so every platform draws it in its own fallback: 7.94px on this
  Mac and about 12.47px on GitHub (its label ended 4.53px further right against
  the same cell edge).
- Widened the column rather than changing letter-spacing or padding. Those
  rules are shared by every List header, and a narrower EFFORT would look
  unlike its neighbours. 6rem gives the glyph room up to about 1.58em. The
  extra 0.5rem comes out of SUMMARY, as the 5.5rem did.
- The test gains a check rather than a tolerance. The old check only measured
  this platform's fonts, so it passed on a Mac with 3.8px spare while Linux
  overflowed. The new one fails on a Mac at 5.5rem with `label 803.765625 past
  cell 803`, against GitHub's `803.734375`. So the Mac now catches what only
  Linux caught. It keeps the existing half-pixel for rounding and adds none.
- Pinned the plan-limit tests to UTC on fixed instants instead of "today at
  noon" in the host zone. The expected words are then literals, and the host's
  clock and zone cannot change them. Shown under UTC+14 and UTC−11 host zones.
