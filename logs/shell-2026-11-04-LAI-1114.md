## 2026-11-04T00:45:00+05:30 — LAI-1114

**What changed**
- cli/src/agent-launcher-service.ts — `installService` is async: after
  `bootout` it polls `launchctl print gui/<uid>/ai.laika.launcher` (40 × 250 ms)
  until the job is gone, retries a `bootstrap` answered with 5 or 37 after
  0.5, 1 and 2 s, and on failure prints `MANUAL_BOOTSTRAP` and returns false
  (enable exits 1). `ServiceIo` gained `sleep`.
- cli/src/agent-launcher.ts — new `AuditEvent` `skipped`; an owner lookup that
  returned nothing is audited `skipped`/`owner_unknown`, `owner_mismatch`
  stays `refused`.
- cli/src/agent-launcher-watch.ts — `skipped` is shown: `skipped a check: could
  not reach the board to confirm the owner (will retry)`.
- cli/test/agent-launcher-service.test.ts — rig takes a `launchctl` answerer and
  records sleeps; six LAI-1114 tests (linger then gone, bounded wait, 5 once,
  37 once, persistent failure prints the command, other errors not retried).
- cli/test/agent-launcher.test.ts, cli/test/agent-launcher-watch.test.ts — the
  skipped event, audited once and rendered.
- cli/README.md — the re-enable wait and retry, the manual command, `skipped`.

**Decisions**
- A new event kind `skipped` with the existing `reason: owner_unknown`, rather
  than a new reason under `refused` — the event itself then says nothing was
  refused, and the reason word anything already greps keeps its meaning.
- Poll by count with an injected sleep, not a wall clock — the tests run
  instantly and the bound is still ~10 s for real.
- A job still present after the bound gets a bootstrap anyway; the retry
  then covers it, and a real failure still ends in the manual command.

**Discovered**
- `ownerOf` returns `undefined` for a network drop and for a board answer
  without an id alike, so the watch line says "could not reach" for both. The
  second is rare; not filed.
