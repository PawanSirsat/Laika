did: `attentionItems` and `repoRows` re-derived three rules and differed

helper *Update available* listed where Developer access lists only *Update
required*. Now:

  underneath); a setup is listed when that chip is *No block found* or
  *Update available*. Admin wording: *Recorded by them* for *Recorded by you*.

  health line agree for five tools. A mutation back to the old tool rule
  reddens both.
