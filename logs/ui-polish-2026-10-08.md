**Decisions**
- Fixed the empty state in the List's container, not in `EmptyState`/`states.css` — the component centres its own content correctly; the List placed it in a flex row. Changing the shared style would have touched a dozen screens for one screen's bug.
- Chips hide with `visibility` while the popover is open rather than unmounting — unmounting moved the table under the popover on every open and close.
- Kept the yes/no options as `<input type="checkbox">` stretched transparent over each pill rather than `aria-pressed` buttons — same semantics, and the existing tests that `.check()` them still describe the control.
- Chip removal for `sprint` writes `sprint=all` (the popover's "Any"), not a delete, which on the Board would mean "the active sprint" (LAI-713).
