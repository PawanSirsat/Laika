**Decisions**
- The trigger is a `<button>` with `role="combobox"`, not a `<div>`: every `<label>` that wrapped or pointed at a `<select>` still names it (measured in Chrome: a label-wrapped combobox button is named by the label alone) and it can be disabled.
- The panel is portalled to `<body>`: the Filter popover scrolls and is slid with `translate`, either of which clips or re-anchors a fixed panel inside it. The price is an explicit `isInDropdownPanel()` check in outside-click handlers; a mutation that skips it turns "a click in the panel" red on both screens.
- The trigger's reset is under `:where(.dd-trigger)` (zero specificity) and sites use `variant="bare"` + their own class, so each control keeps the look its `<select>` had whatever order the CSS loads in.
- Space chooses only without a search box (it types a space in one); Enter chooses in both.
- Took LAI-726, not the brief's LAI-725: the §3 sweep found 725 on `build-ui-polish` (`LAI-725-timeline-space-bar-filters-do-nothing.md`).
- The task-panel slice (TaskMeta, Dependencies, Subtasks, AssignControl and their CSS/tests) was built by a forked sub-agent in this worktree, uncommitted; reviewed here before commit — one comment overclaimed how Chrome names a label-wrapped combobox and was corrected.

**Proof each new test is real** (old files from `master` swapped in by name, restored with `git restore --source=HEAD` in a trap, tree checked clean after each)
- filter-dropdown.test.ts on the old BoardToolbar/board-toolbar.css/BoardScreen: 17 of 17 fail.
