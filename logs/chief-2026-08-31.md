**What happened.** SHELL diagnosed why LAI-224's task file arrived unfinished:

**Decisions**

- **Reproduced it in a scratch repo before writing it into the working
  agreement.** A mechanism claim that is about to become a rule for three

- **Reframed it from a builder error to a protocol defect.** §2 said *"Tick every

  carelessness. Twice is not carelessness when the document tells you to do it
  that way. The fix is the order, not a reminder to be careful.
- **Put the tell in `/review` as well as the rule in §2.** A rule prevents it

  silently repairs the record it is checking destroys the evidence that the
