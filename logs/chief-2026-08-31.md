  branch reports itself as diverged from a name that no longer exists.

  `[[:>:]]` are the BSD equivalents. Same lesson as the browser probes: when a
  check says the work is done, first prove the check can see the thing it claims
  to have removed.
- A bare `s/pm/chief/g` would have turned `pnpm` into `pnchief` throughout the
  repo. Guarded against it with an explicit grep before applying — worth keeping
  as a habit for any repo-wide substitution of a short token.

## 12:40 — [ops] Filed M3, and found two sessions wearing one identity

**What changed**

- `.tasks/backlog/LAI-402` … `LAI-413` — the M3 task set, twelve files.
- `docs/ROADMAP.md` — M3 section corrected and cross-referenced to the tasks.

**Why now.** The backlog held 37 items, **none of them p1, and not one building
a feature**. M1, M2 and M2.5 are built; M3 — the milestone that makes an agent a
first-class member of the board, which is the entire point of Laika — had
`server/src/mcp/` not existing, no tokens route or service, and nothing filed.
The board had run out of forward work and the reason was mine.

**Decisions**

- **Split the transport (LAI-406) from the tools (LAI-407, LAI-408).** Transport
  bugs and tool bugs look nothing alike. Debugging them in one task is how a
  milestone slips, and neither half is reviewable while the other is in flight.
- **Split the four read tools from the six write tools.** Reads must not mutate
  — including `last_used_at` — and that is a property worth testing on its own,
  against a task that contains no writes at all.
- **Named `@modelcontextprotocol/sdk` in LAI-406 and nowhere else.** CLAUDE.md §5
  forbids a dependency a task does not name; naming it in exactly one task means
  a package appearing anywhere else in M3 is visible as a defect rather than as
  a judgement call.
- **Filed LAI-404 and LAI-405 as their own tasks rather than folding them into
  the tools.** `get_project_context` serves `context_md` and `log_unlisted_work`
  writes `unlisted_work`, and **neither surface has a REST route at all** — the
  columns exist, nothing reads or writes them. Discovering that mid-tool would
  have meant a builder either widening their own task or stopping. Better found
  now, by reading §6.4 against `routes/`, than at the point of use.
- **Sequenced LAI-411 first for SHELL, with no dependency.** `ActivityView`
  already carries `actor_kind`, so badging is buildable today. Otherwise SHELL
  parks behind the entire server chain — which is what happened during the UI
  push and is what the owner was angry about.
- **Made every M3 server task p1.** They are the milestone. Eight p1 items is a
  lot, but a board whose highest priority is p2 is a board with nothing urgent,
  and that was not true.

**Discovered**

  nine of them have REST twins. The count has been wrong since
  `log_unlisted_work` was added. Corrected in place with the correction recorded,
  not silently — a milestone whose scope line understates it by two tools is how
  work goes missing.
- **SPEC §14 q7 (the `context_md` size bound) is already answered in code** —
  `routes/projects.ts` caps at 100,000. LAI-404 settles it there rather than
  inventing a number. I should close q7 in the spec once LAI-404 lands.
