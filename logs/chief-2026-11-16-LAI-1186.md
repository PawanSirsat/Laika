**What changed**
- server/web/test/browser/my-day-span.test.ts, "Day is a layer…": the test now
  clicks `other`, a day of the current week that is not today. That is
  yesterday when `mondayOf(YESTERDAY) === mondayOf(TODAY)`, and otherwise
  tomorrow, which only happens on a Monday. The old version clicked yesterday's
  column in this week's view, so it timed out every Monday.
- server/web/test/browser/my-day-span.test.ts, "Month: its weeks, a day nothing
  reached says —…": the test now opens a month whose grid has a past day the
  fixture holds nothing for. That is today's month, or last month when today's
  grid starts within a day of today. It takes the latest such day, so its week
  falls inside the fixture's ±21 days, and opens that day's week number instead
  of today's. The old version failed with "the grid has a past day with nothing
  in it" on the 1st when it is a Monday or a Tuesday, and on the 2nd when it is
  a Tuesday. Before the fix it went red pinned to 2027-02-01, on exactly that
  assertion.

**Decisions**
- On a Monday the "other day" is tomorrow, a future day. It stays a day of the
  current week, as the criterion asks, and the Day page opens a future day like
  any other. Picking last week's Sunday instead would have left the week, which
  is the very assumption being removed.
- The month test falls back to last month rather than adding a quiet day to the
  fixture. `weekData()` is shared by twenty-odd files, and changing it would
  move every one of them.
- To pin TODAY, `my-day-clock.ts`'s `TODAY` line was swapped temporarily by a
  script that checked its anchor, then copied the file back and confirmed the
  sha256 matched (`e81998c36145` every time). Nothing about the pinning is
  committed.
