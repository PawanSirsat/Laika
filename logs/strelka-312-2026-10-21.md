**Decisions**
- Reached `code` and `italic` through the kit's `addExtensions`. The
  alternative was importing `@tiptap/extension-code` / `-italic`, which are
  not direct dependencies of `server/web`; adding them needs a task (§5).
  `extendMarkSchema` is ruled out by core's source (`getSchema`, 3.32.0):
  core spreads it **before** the mark's own `excludes`, so `'_'` wins.
- Made `_italic_` round-trip instead of leaving it refused. Every imported
  card opens with `_Imported … from the repo's `.tasks/` folder._`, so with
  only the parse fixed the owner's card would still open as Markdown, one
  line further down. The delimiter is spelling, so the comparison ignores it.
  An edited block that cannot keep `_` (`x_b_y`) is written `*` instead of
  becoming unwritable.
- Left C (bullet wraps) to PR #13 and D (task-item wraps) to LAI-302. Both
  were already filed by someone else, and §3 says not to do discovered work
  that is already owned.
- Filing and claiming were briefly two commits. I folded them into one
  before pushing, as the brief and §2 ask.
