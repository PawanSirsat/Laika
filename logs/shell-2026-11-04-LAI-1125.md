**Decisions**
- One token per face (`--font-face-*`) and `--font-ui` pointing at one — rather than family names in the data-font blocks and again in the picker — so the picker previews through the same value the UI uses.
- `font-synthesis-weight: none` on the single-weight faces rather than mapping the weight tokens: 935 `font-weight` declarations in `src/**/*.css` go through five tokens, and remapping them per face would also flatten Plus Jakarta's real bolds the moment the blocks were wrong. The cost is that bold text in those faces shows at regular weight; the picker says so.
- No `size-adjust` for Poppins: it needs owning the @font-face rule instead of the package's, and the browser test measures no card or panel overflowing at 390 or 1440 in any face. The picker notes that long titles cut off sooner.
- The quick *Switch to dark/light* row stays in the menu beside *Appearance…*; one click is what most readers want.
