**Decisions**
- Day stays outside the stage, so it is byte-for-byte the page it was. A zoom
  into a day plays the coarse half on the stage and then hands over; arriving
  from Day plays the other half once the period has loaded.
- The leaving layer is never cancelled, only unmounted, so it cannot flash
  back for a frame. Only the arriving layer's animation is cancelled.
- Week reads 7 × `GET /me/time`, because it needs the blocks; Month and Year
  read LAI-1173's `GET /me/time/days`. A period read once is cached while the
  stage is up, so zooming back out is instant, and read fresh next time.
- Wider views hide Add time, Fill gaps, Confirm day and Day note, and the N/T
  keys do nothing there. The timer stays, as in the design.
- A profile's embedded day is Day only.

## 2026-11-12T12:40:00Z — LAI-1174, CI round 1

PR #334's first run was red in two places:
- `test/screen-header.test.ts` *"no in-app screen renders its own top-level
  heading"*: the phone head's period title was an `<h1>`. It is a `<p>` now.
- `browser/my-day-span.test.ts`: Month, Year and the keys found 0 cells.
  `settled()` waited for the layer to mount, not for its data; on CI's slower
  machine the skeleton was still up. It now waits for a card without
  `aria-busy`. That exposed a fixture gap the local pass had hidden: ← in
  Month puts `?day=` a month back, past the fixture's ±21 days of
  `GET /me/time`, and the page's own day read then fails. The extra days are
  served now.

**Discovered:** on the wider views the page still reads the day in the address
for its header, so a failed day read replaces the whole page, stage included.
That is harmless against a real server, which serves any day, but the stage
does not need it. Worth decoupling if it ever bites.
