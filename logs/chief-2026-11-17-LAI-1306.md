  refuses `/events`, a refused stream is forgotten so the next subscriber

  counts every source ever created. That move exposed the real defect: the
  pill read CONNECTING on a healthy shared stream because `open`/`ready` are
  said once per connection and it had become a late joiner. Replay disabled →
  the test goes red on the pill; the unit tests go red on the dropped and
  first-subscriber cases.

  Create or the eye would also fit, but removes a feature from a screen; the
  chevron is decoration on the bar (the pill is the control, as it was before
  LAI-1223). 17px spare at 390 with *On leave*; screenshots both themes looked
  right.

  mark and avatar are `display: none` there by LAI-1227's rule, while a
  displayed control squeezed to nothing still fails *has no width*. It now
