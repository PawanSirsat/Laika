**Decisions**
- Each lead-in is pinned per place rather than made identical across the
  three — they legitimately differ (each names the other two, and the usage
  has its one-line summary), and making them identical would have been a
  wording change nobody asked for.
- The N2 sentence says *"every other rule above applies to it as written"*
  and then names the cases, rather than listing every condition under which
  it records — a list would be one more place to drift; the general clause is
  true by construction, the named cases are what the review found and what
  the new test pins.
- The watchdog's marker is asserted immediately after `run()`, before any
  other assertion, so a slow run cannot let it fire between the run and the
  check. The FIFO test takes about 1 s on Belka, so 10 s is ~10x margin.
- Mutants ran on throwaway copies (rsync of the tracked files, or
  `git archive eee6c4d9` for the before column), killed by process group on a
  hang, so no mutation could be left behind in the worktree.

**Discovered**
- The older test *"usage errors are 64, never a finding; --help is 0"* already
