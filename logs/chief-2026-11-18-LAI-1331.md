**Decisions**
- F1, the skipped-mailbox case: **ignore a span not seen for a day**, rather than retire on
  connection off. The sync stamps `last_seen_at` on every span it reads, every 15 minutes, so

  keeps failing, with no write from a read. Connection off is covered by the enabled gate, and
  a switch turned back on shows nothing older than a day until the sync reads it again.

  writes nothing when it is gone, so a sync finishing after the delete cannot put a span back.
- No index for retention: a person has a handful of spans. Said in §4.107.
