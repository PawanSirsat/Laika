**Decisions**
- Tokens in their own sheet, not theme.css — open PRs own theme.css, and motion
  is not themed. Names/values match PR #291's block so they merge to one; the
  guard ("no other stylesheet declares a motion token") goes red until the
  second to land deletes its copy. Intentional.
- Glide stays 200ms `ease`: D-071 fixes both. Rejected retuning it to the base
  token.
- Global rule = tokens to 0ms, not `animation: none` everywhere — a 0s
  animation lands its end state, so every state change still shows. The
  arrival glow keeps its 3.6s (it is the information) and its local
  `steps(1, end)` block, the guard's named exemption.
- `--transition` (colour) stays in theme.css untouched; a guard pins its
  duration to `--duration-fast` instead of editing a file #291 rewrites.
- confirm-dialog.css's two animation lines are exempt, not migrated: both sit
  beside lines #291 rewrites and would conflict. The exemption proves itself
  stale once they move.
- Dropdown panel fades with opacity only — `Dropdown.tsx` measures its own box
  to place itself; a mid-flight transform would be "corrected" into a wrong
  place.
- Skill advice applied: strong ease-out for entrances (`emil-design-eng`,
  `animate`), transform-origin from the trigger, press scale 0.97 with an
  instant transition (`review-animations`), cut layout-property and
  keyboard-driven motion (`remove-animations`, `animation-performance`),
  transitions not keyframes where interruptible. Rejected: Motion/springs (CSP
  and no new deps — spring-like is a cubic-bezier token); adding a drawer exit
  (needs delayed unmount in the host #289 owns); rewriting paint-bound pulses
  and the sheen to transforms (markup changes, low value — noted in the audit);
  `scale(0)` sign-in tick (auth.css overlaps #291).
