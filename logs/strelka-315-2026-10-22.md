## 2026-10-22 — LAI-315

**What changed**
- cli/src/activity-usage.ts — `claudeDay` collects each dispatched builder's
  (skip line tagged `omnigent`) reply times and tokens per card. New
  `workedBuckets` turns those times into seconds per five minutes, sent as
  the day's `dispatched` (`{ key, task?, from, seconds[] }`). A dispatched
  key is no longer in `excluded`. `CACHE_V` 3 → 4 (the cache gained `b`).
  `cache.skip` records the `omnigent` word, so a session that gains it is

**Decisions**
- **Transcripts, not OTel.** Measured from inside this dispatched builder:
  its environment has no `CLAUDE_CODE_ENABLE_TELEMETRY` and no `OTEL_*`.
  Builders inherit Omnigent's environment, not `~/.zshrc`, so a builder's
  lane can only come from its transcript, which the collector already reads.
- **Worked time = gaps of at most 15 minutes between replies.** Replies
  further apart are two stretches of work. Fifteen minutes, not five, because
  a builder sits silent through a full gate run (about ten minutes). A lone
  reply counts nothing. It is an estimate from timestamps, and the lane is
  the only place it shows.
- **No schema change.** A bucket of a key in `token_session_skips` can only
  have come from `dispatched`: the receiver drops a skipped key's points, and
  `excluded` drops its buckets. A `dispatched` column would have needed a
  migration, a SPEC §4.57 row and a `COLUMNS_NOT_IN_SPEC` exemption, all for
  a flag nothing reads yet.
- **The card tie is one token in `token_session_tasks`.** The lane only asks
  whether a session's split is above zero. Nothing sums it, because a
  dispatched session keeps no `agent_time_days` to split. The comment on
  `storeDispatched` says so.
- **No `server/web` change.** The lane already draws minutes only and never
  adds them to hours, so nothing there needed a visual distinction to meet
  the decision.
- **A day's `dispatched` replaces that day's buckets for its keys** (per day,
  not per session), because a report never splits a day but can split a
  session across midnight.

**Discovered**
- `docs/SPEC.md` §4.57 says the lane's rows come only from the OTLP receiver
  and that a left-out session gets none. §6.4 does not name `dispatched`.
  Both are CHIEF's to amend; LAI-316 (the decision record for LAI-314 and
  LAI-315) is where it belongs. No drift guard reads §6.4's usage-day fields,
  so nothing went red.
