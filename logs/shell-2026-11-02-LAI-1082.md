**Decisions**
- Step 2 and 3 are reachable by address without a grant (with a "Grant required" note and
  the record button's own gate) — forcing step 1 would hide existing records of a tool
  whose grant was later removed; only *Continue* is gated, as the design draws it.
- An address at `done` with no record is rewritten to `step=3` — the done card says what
  was set up, and with nothing recorded it would have nothing honest to say.
- The access read and the records are held against the (tool, project) pair they were
  read for rather than remounting the wizard on a picker change — remounting took focus
  off the picker that changed it.
- The design's "Repository name or folder (for your reference)" field is not built: it is
  a demo label nothing stores; a repository's name comes from `laika project check`'s
  `repo_label`.
- The design's two checkboxes on step 3 are replaced by the two real ways to record (the
  plan's call): a check from the device (the only *Checked*) or the reader's word.
