# SHELL — 2026-11-11 — LAI-1132

## 2026-11-11 — LAI-1132 follow-up: calm, simple ambient background

The owner's feedback, word for word: "for the background the theme doesn't go well keep it
simple". The navy-to-periwinkle gradient, the haze discs and the rim arcs were too much.

**What changed**
- server/web/src/styles/theme.css: the ambient tokens are now `--ambient-base` and
  `--ambient-peak` only.
  - Dark is brand Ink with one faint, large lift toward the top:
    `linear-gradient(180deg, #0f1422 0%, #0b0f1a 60%)`.
  - Light is Paper settling into a faint Fog at the bottom:
    `linear-gradient(180deg, #f4f7fb 40%, #edf1f7 100%)`.
  - `--ambient-peak` is the extreme stop in each theme (`#0f1422` / `#edf1f7`).
  - Removed `--ambient-orb-1`/`-2`/`-3`, `--ambient-arc` and `--ambient-rim`.
  - Over a near-flat ground, glass is quieter:
    - blur is 12/16/20px for the three levels, down from 18/24/30, and 6px for the scrim, down
      from 8;
    - saturate is 1.2, down from 1.8;
    - the sheen is one faint stop with no lavender;
    - the selected glow and the focus-ring halo are softer.
  - The focus ring's two solid rings are unchanged.
- server/web/src/components/glass/glass.css: `body::before` is the single ambient layer with
  one background, `var(--ambient-base)`. The two arc gradients and three radial hazes are gone.
  There never were keyframes, and there are none now. The reduced-transparency and
  forced-colours fallbacks are unchanged and still hide the layer.
- server/web/src/theme/token-list.ts: the Ambient group lists only the two tokens that remain.
- server/web/test/tokens.test.ts: the orb-stack worst-case test is replaced by a test of the
  new behaviour. Per theme it checks that:
  - `--ambient-base` is exactly one linear gradient, with no radial, conic or rgba, and one or
    two stops;
  - each stop is within 1.1:1 of `--bg-canvas`;
  - `--ambient-peak` is one of the stops and the extreme of them;
  - the removed orb, arc and rim tokens are absent.
- server/web/test/browser/glass.test.ts: on the sign-in page the layer must be one
  `linear-gradient(` with no `radial-gradient` and `animation-name: none`; before, the test
  required radial plus linear. When `GLASS_SHOTS` is set, the task-drawer test now also writes
  side-panel screenshots for dark/light at 1440/390.

**Decisions**
- I kept one tonal shift rather than going fully flat. The brief allowed one, and it keeps the
  glass chrome from reading as a cut-out. The test holds each stop to 1.1:1 of the canvas, so
  the shift cannot grow back into a feature.
- Glass alphas are unchanged. They were set against the old, brighter peak, so they clear AA
  over the darker peak with more room.
