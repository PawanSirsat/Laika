**Decisions**
- The p50 is calibrated on the model's past errors as well as the p80 — the
  R&D's §5.3 recipe. Calibrating p80 alone left the "likely" figure ×1.65 too
  long on recent work (agents got faster than their history), with a range
  that looked narrow around it. Same correction in the replay; the method's
  error on the board's history went ×1.74 → ×1.65.
- The back-test over the board's history is a test with a fixed cut-off
  (finished before 2026-10-17), not "whatever `.tasks/done/` holds" — D-037: on
  fixed data, beating the median is a property of the code; on growing data it
  would be a contingent fact.
- An override changes the size shown, not the hours, and accuracy measures
  only the model's frozen forecast — rather than scaling the range by the
  person's level, which would let an override move the figure it is scored on.
- A done or cancelled task keeps its estimate; the refit fills one that has
  none, once. Rather than re-estimating history every night.
- Recompute is inline in the write (≤ 0.5 ms measured) rather than queued, so
  the board never shows a size older than the activity row announcing a change
  — a background recompute would need its own SSE signal, i.e. a new verb.
- The override uses the app's `Dropdown`, not a native `<select>` — LAI-726's
  guard refuses one.
- Nested response types are named interfaces on both sides (`SizeOverride`,
  `FrozenEstimate`, `BacktestFigures`, `ClientAccuracy`): `view-type-drift`'s
  field reader takes a multi-line inline object's fields as top-level fields.
- The List's Size column comes out of Summary rather than raising the table's
  `min-width`, which made a laptop scroll further.
- Migration filed as 0036 (reserved) at journal idx 35, its snapshot chained to
  0034's. drizzle-kit numbers by journal length, so whichever of LAI-769 /
  LAI-771 lands second must regenerate on top of the other's, as the task says.

**Discovered**
- Seeding two months of history in one process without the nightly refit froze
  most forecasts from a 10-task model (×2.16, p80 45%); with a refit per
  simulated day it is ×1.78, bias 1.01, p80 77%. A warm fit is only refitted
  nightly, so a young project growing fast inside one day forecasts from
  yesterday's model. A growth-triggered refit is a phase-2 option — not filed.
- Pre-existing, not caused here: the List's `min-width` (71.75rem) already
  overflows its card at 1366 px (Updated clipped), and `.list-col-key` wraps a
  three-digit key (`LAI-131`) onto two lines. Not filed — UI polish is on the
  strategy's stop list; left for the orchestrator to triage.
- The Laika MCP has no search, so the board card mirroring LAI-771 could not be
  found; none was created on the production board.

**Decisions**
- A person's size is their write, not a derived figure: when it cannot be
  stored the whole `PATCH` is refused and nothing of it saved — rather than
  saving the rest and dropping the size behind a 200, or saving the rest
  behind an error, which was the bug.
- The freeze retry is "the newest model row at or before `started_at`", not
  "started after the upgrade" — it needs no upgrade timestamp (drizzle records
  a migration's generation time, not when it ran) and cannot pick an estimate
  written after work began.
- Both new transactions take the write lock up front (`immediate`): a
  deferred one that reads first can lose its snapshot to another connection.

  full parallel gate (12 ms against a 4 ms bare interpreter) and passed alone

  LAI-768 family — not filed.
