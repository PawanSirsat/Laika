**Decisions**
- **A flag, not a status.** The task keeps its status underneath, so Restore puts it back
  exactly. A hard delete is impossible anyway (§4.8 append-only rows with RESTRICT,
  `task_estimates`, BCEA time, numbers are `MAX+1`).
- **No pair CHECK on `tasks`.** A table-level CHECK makes drizzle-kit rebuild `tasks`, as the
  `deliverables` comment already says. The one writer sets both columns.
  - The migration's `ADD archived_by` was hand-given `ON DELETE set null`, which drizzle-kit
    drops on an `ADD COLUMN` (0062 and 0078 did the same).
- **Throughput and cycle time keep archived tasks.** They are history: what was finished stays
  finished. People will archive old done work, and that must not dent the chart.
  - Counts, forecast, workload and queues leave archived tasks out.
- **Comments are read-only too.** An email reply to an archived task's thread is therefore
  dropped `refused`, the reason an archived space already uses, and never made a new task.
- **The Archived count is in the panel, not on the toolbar button.** A count on the button
  would be one more read on every board load (`/tasks/summary`), and `store-requests` counts
  those. I changed my own criterion's wording to match, before review.
- **The drawer stays open after Archive**, on the task read by id, so the banner and Restore are
  where the reader is.
- **Bulk Archive confirms with a toast, not the bar's report.** The archived rows leave the
  List, and the bar and its report go with them, which is what the first browser run caught.

**For CHIEF: a decision to record (supersedes D-067 item 3)**
> D-067 item 3 said "Delete is Cancel … a real delete is a separate decision". The owner made
> it on 2026-11-19 (LAI-1353–1355):
> - **Archive** is a flag on the task, `archived_at`. It puts the task away whole and restorably
>   (any task; whoever may edit; signed in only).
> - **Delete** is a soft delete into the space's Trash, restorable for 30 days and then scrubbed
>   to a tombstone that keeps the key and activity (space admin any task, member their own;
>   signed in only).
> - Both leave every board and queue through one `liveTask()` filter, held by a census.
> - Cancel stays a status.
