**Decisions**
- Agent tokens treated as always pinned (to their grants) and refused `token.create_own` / `token.revoke_own` — otherwise an agent could mint its owner's legacy token and escape its grants.
- All agent management session only — a token, an agent's own included, must not widen a grant or rotate a sibling's credential.
- Grant narrowing lives only in `can()`; the resolver does not also narrow `projectIds`, so there is one place to break (mutation check goes red).
- `tokens.agent_id` ON DELETE cascade, hand-added to the ALTER — drizzle-kit dropped it, and set-null would widen a token to its owner.
- No new activity verb (three owners); `token.created` / `token.revoked` reused with their payloads unchanged.
- `harness` without a CHECK — a new client would otherwise rebuild a table tokens cascade from.
