**Decisions**
- The undo id travels as a response header, so no response body or status
  changes (dismiss and delete stay 204).
- "Counted twice" is measured in minutes, pro-rated per entry over its span —
  not span overlap — because the collector's parallel-task blocks overlap by
  design with each holding a share of the stretch.
- The same rule covers timed manual entries (create, edit, undo of a delete):
  Add time into a gap could otherwise overrun into confirmed time.

**Discovered**

  between the toast and the click lets it take back the wrong thing.
- Moving a typed Solidtime fixture: its overlap was incidental, now refused.

## 2026-10-20T22:30:17Z — LAI-831 review round 2

**What changed**
- `server/src/services/time-entries.ts` — the pro-rated rule
  `assertNotCountedTwice` is replaced. `assertWithinSpan` gives `422
  minutes_over_span`. `stretchAround` and `assertFitsTheClock` judge the
  whole transitive stretch by the clock. `assertLeftBehindFits` judges what an
  entry edit leaves behind.
- `server/src/services/time-drafts.ts` — draft span/length and single confirm
  use the new rule. *Confirm day* writes every entry, then judges each one on
  the final state: all-or-nothing, naming its block.
- `server/web/src/routes/screens/my-day/draft-bulk.ts` — a refused
  *Confirm day* says nothing was confirmed, names the block, and gives its
  stretch.
- `server/test/http/routes/time-my-day-v2.test.ts`,
  `server/web/test/routes/screens/my-day/draft-bulk.test.ts` — the
  round-2 probes, committed red first.
- `.tasks/review/LAI-831-…md` — Review round 2 note.

**Decisions**
- *Confirm day* is all-or-nothing, not "confirm what fits". A partial day
  would depend on which block was judged first, and the brief asked for
  order-independence. The day either fits or is named.
- *Confirm day* judges after writing every entry, with minutes 0. It does not
  judge block by block before each write with the block's own minutes, because
  that sees the day half-built and is the order-dependence being removed. A
  single confirm still judges before its one write; the result is the same.

**Discovered**
- An entry edit was only judged where it landed. Shrinking or moving the long
  entry that held a stretch open left the overlapping blocks counting twice.
  Fixed in this task (test red first): it is the same rule in a place the
  brief named.
- A delete, or an Undo that retires an entry, can leave a stretch over in the
  same way. It is not refused. That is a decision for CHIEF and is flagged in
  the task note. No task filed, because it belongs to this review.
