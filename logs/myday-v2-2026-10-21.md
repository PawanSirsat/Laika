**Decisions**
- Undo is a server journal of before-images, not inverse calls from the page —
  nothing the page holds could restore a held block's collector, a split
  block's id or a draft's origin, and "exactly" was the criterion.
- An entry cannot be deleted while kept (§4.48 trigger), so Undo of a confirm
  is a `deleted` revision and a re-confirm revives the one entry a draft has
  (`time_entries_source_ref_unique`) — never a second entry.
- Existing `can()` actions for breaks and undo — a break is as private as a
  draft; no new §3.1 row, so no SPEC crossing beyond LAI-836's §4/§6.4.
- Undo replaced LAI-810's two bulk asks; the inspector's Confirm stays
  disabled until a card or General (the old row Confirm silently made General).
- Typed minutes and typed split times gave way to −/+, drag and Split in two;
  Add time keeps a typed length with LAI-830's *Reads as*.
- Week bars are shares of the week's busiest day — no fixed day length, so no
  implied target (E).
- G: the counts case — a badge per block; SIGNALS and AGENTS lanes filed.
