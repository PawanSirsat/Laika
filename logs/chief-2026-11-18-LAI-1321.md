**Decisions**
- **No completion deadline to handle.** Icon ask/complete is stateless (D-152): the only clock is the presigned `PUT`'s ten minutes, spent when *Use this* is pressed. A held image stays completable until Save.
- **The preview is a `data:` URL of the cropped bytes**, not a `blob:` URL: `img-src` allows `'self' data:` and the store origin, never `blob:`, so a `blob:` preview would be blank in production.
- **The Icon row shows only to someone who may also edit the space's settings**: Save lives in General's footer, and an emoji goes in the same `PATCH`, which needs `project.settings.edit`.
- **D-152 is not edited**: it never said "saves at once" (SPEC §11.4.2.1 did, and is rewritten), and decisions are append-only.
- **White and black backgrounds are canvas colour names, not tokens**: they are the stored image's own pixels, the same in every theme; the swatches paint the same value inline. The space-colour background reads the theme's `--space-<colour>`.
- **The frame is fixed at 15rem** (`FRAME_PX`), on a phone too: a drag then moves the image by exactly the pointer's pixels.

**Discovered**
- Laika has three themes — dark (bare `:root`), light and black — so every "in dark" rule is `:root:not([data-theme='light'])`.
