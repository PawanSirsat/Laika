## 2026-11-10T13:05:00Z — LAI-1170 — review round 1 (CHIEF, PR #311 on eddab356)

**What changed.** `server/web/src/routes/screens/organisation/WorkTab.tsx`:
a refetch of the same page keeps its rows until the new page lands
(`pageShown` ref; only another filter, size or cursor starts from the
loading state); the bar is one `bar` element rendered while anything is
selected **or** a run's report is up, in the list branch and the two empty
branches; `scopeStatuses`; a refused statuses or sprints read is dropped from
the asked-set so the next selection reads again; `aria-selected` gone from
the row. `list/BulkBar.tsx`: `statusesLoading` / `sprintsLoading`,
`LOADING_SCOPE`, `heldFor` — a held action says *Loading statuses…* with
`aria-busy` while its one space's list is on its way, *Choose one space…*
only when there is no one space; the label never changes. `work-tab.css`:
`.work-row-selected` after `.work-row:hover`. `test/browser/
organisation-work.test.ts`: the bulk run's stub answers page one re-ordered
after the run (the changed rows gone, page two's rows up), the report with
its count and *Dismiss* is asserted after the refetch, a `MutationObserver`
asserts the loading state never returned; the tint is asserted as computed
background under the pointer; a gated statuses route that refuses once
proves the loading hold and the retry; the viewer-everywhere case waits for
the permissions answer, the *Total* tile and the pager first.

**Decisions.**
- The report outlives the selection, not the other way round: after the
  server's own ordering moves the changed rows off the page, "2 updated, 1
  refused" is the one thing the reader is waiting for, and *Dismiss* (or ×)
  is theirs. The List never met this because the board holds every task.
- The never-unmounted instrument is a `MutationObserver` installed after the
  first rows, not `watchFor` from page open: the first load is meant to
  show the loading state, and a from-the-start watcher could not tell the two
  apart.
- A refused scope read is forgotten rather than remembered as failed: the
  next selection of that space is the natural retry, and nothing else has
  to be told.

**Discovered.** The stub returning the same rows in the same order after a
run was *a fixture cleaner than reality*: the server's `asc(updated_at)` is
what moves the rows, and the test passed by never modelling it. Written into
the stub's comment so the next bulk test starts from the server's behaviour.
