**Decisions.**
- The moved `.list-menu-item:focus-visible` rule drops its `outline: none`
  for an explicit accent ring: `test/states.test.ts` scans every `.css` in
  `src/components/` and refuses `outline: none` outside `:not(:focus-visible)`.
  Everything else in `bulk-bar.css` is the List's text, selectors unchanged.
- `runBulkAction` takes an optional `apply` (default `applyAction`) rather
  than the plan's `keyOf`: the tasks carry their keys, and injecting the
  request is what lets the unit test pin the phase sequence without a server.
- Work's selection is keyed by `queryKey#pageIndex`, not by `queryKey` alone:
  a server page is a window on the server's order, and a selection made on
  page two names rows page one does not show. `effectiveSelection` would
  prune them anyway; the key makes the intent explicit.
- The `.work-head` keeps its labels `aria-hidden` through a `display:
  contents` wrapper so the select-all checkbox can live in the head without
  sitting under an `aria-hidden` ancestor.
- The empty-state copy left `screen-copy.ts` rather than being re-keyed:
  `routes.test.ts` requires copy per route, and a tab is not a route.
- `BulkBar` finds its card by `.list-card, .work-card` rather than a new
  data attribute, so the List's markup and its tests do not change.
