**Decisions**
- **Two columns, not one:**
  - The key is ciphertext in `smtp_json_enc` (`{password}`).
  - The host, port, login, From and the key's last four are plain JSON in `smtp_settings`.
  - Building a view therefore never decrypts. That's LAI-447's rule for `ai_key_last4`,
    kept, and it's why the view can show `key_last4` without the key.
- **The outbox body is encrypted and short-lived:** an invite or reset mail carries a
  working one-time link, and §13.1 stores no token in the clear. `body_enc` is cleared the
  moment a row is final; a suppressed row is written without one.
- **Suppression is decided at enqueue and again at send time.** The switch is asked first,
  so off means off. A row queued while email was on becomes `email_off` if the switch is
  turned off before it goes.
- **Classifying timeouts:** nodemailer reports every timeout as `ETIMEDOUT`, and its
  `command` says `CONN` even during `DATA`. So the classifier reads `timeoutType`: only
  connect, greeting and TLS-upgrade timeouts are safe to retry. Read from nodemailer
  10.6.0's `smtp-connection`, not assumed.
- **`createApp` and the scheduler default to no transport:** `createApp` refuses every send
  with `ENOTRANSPORT`, and the scheduler does nothing. A test that forgets its recorder can
  never reach a mail server; only `index.ts` passes `smtpTransport()`.
- **No `no_public_url` link:** a link in an email must be absolute. With no
  `LAIKA_PUBLIC_URL`, invites and resets record `no_public_url` while the response still
  carries the relative link, and the card warns.
- **Email routes stay token-holdable:** they use `org.settings.edit`, as the brief said.
  That's the same row the AI key already uses, and a token may hold it.
- **Client reads tolerate an older server:** the web reads `OrgView.email` and
  `{invite, reset}.email` through `emailOn` and `dispatchOf`, which treat an answer without
  them as off. That covers a deploy in flight where the web and the API are a release apart,
  and it spared 22 browser fixtures that stub `GET /org` without the field.
- **Defaults are pre-filled, never stored:** the card is pre-filled with Brevo, 587 and
  `ksage@kvelld.com` / *Ksage* as CHIEF decided. Nothing is stored until Save. Another board
  edits them first.
