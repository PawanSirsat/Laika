### D-055 — I measured the obvious fix and then declined it

CORE's diagnosis was right: three flakes in three workspaces, **one signature**,
*"every timeout in the repo chosen against an idle machine."* The remedy is to
stop running the workspaces concurrently, and it is cheap — **88s parallel, 160s
sequential**, same tree, both `EXIT 0`.

**SHELL's LAI-452 is why I did not take it.** That flake was not a timing
problem: `execFile` reports a failure to *start* through the same callback as a
non-zero exit, and the harness mapped a string errno to `1` — so a machine that
could not fork produced `code: 1` with both streams empty, **byte for byte what a
hook exiting 1 silently looks like.**

**A sequential gate would never have surfaced it.** So the parallel gate is not
the cause of these findings; **it is the instrument that found them**, and the
only place three workspaces compete the way a real machine does.

**LAI-166 settles the "just raise the timeout" answer**: its bound was **already
ten times the solo duration** and it still failed. A bigger multiple is the
calendar-fixture defect wearing a stopwatch.

**I wrote down what would make this wrong** — a flake with no harness bug and no
unjustified bound behind it — rather than resting on three-for-three.
