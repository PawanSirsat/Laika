**Decisions**
- **Not `/capacity`.** It is heavy, and for admins it logs a look per person, so polling it
  from every page would put the admin in everyone's *Who looked*. Not `/presence`: agents
  only, no away.
- **Not grouping heartbeats through their user index.** `GROUP BY user_id` made SQLite walk
  `(user_id, created_at)` over every heartbeat ever kept; `GROUP BY +user_id` reads the hour's
  time range. The plan test caught it.
- **The same statuses as Capacity**, and the reader included (owner's choice). Browsing alone
  is still not "online": §9.3's rule is unchanged.
- **The faces are `<Avatar>`** (single source) inside a ring span; `AvatarGroup` was not
  reused, because the stack is one interactive button and its label carries presence words.
- **The `+N` lozenge** uses `--bg-pill` and `--text-primary`: `--bg-column` vanished on the dark
  bar.
