**Decisions**
- Configured by env, not in the app: an SSO-only instance with a broken IdP is
  recovered by unsetting the variables. An in-app toggle would sit behind the
  sign-in that broke.
- Single tenant enforced at boot (`common`/`organizations`/`consumers`
  refused): with it, Microsoft's email comes from a directory this org runs,
  which is what makes linking by email and matching invites by email safe.
- Invites matched by email, email-addressed only: the SSO flow carries no
  token, so a link invite would be spendable by anyone in the tenant.
- `sign_in` values `email`/`microsoft`, not `password`: a guard keeps the word
  "password" out of the public setup body.
- No new dependency: better-auth 1.7.1 ships the Microsoft provider.
