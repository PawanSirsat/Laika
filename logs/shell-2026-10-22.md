**Decisions**
- Work goes in `HOME`, not a `WORK` group — LAI-796 removed `WORK` and
  `routes.test.ts` forbids it coming back; HOME is the unheaded group of the
  reader's own places, the nearest thing to what the criterion names.
- Rows are drawn in the server's order (oldest change first) and the header
  says so — re-sorting a partly loaded list client-side would misstate it.
- The space filter is `?space=`, not `?project=` — the shell reads `?project=`
  as the current space, and Work is org-level.
- A row opens `/board?project=<slug>&task=<id>` — the existing drawer in the
  task's own space, the way Capacity opens one.
- Names come from `GET /users` (everyone may read it, `member_list.read`),
  including deactivated people; sprint names are read only for spaces on the
  page or chosen in the filter.
- The data hook lives in `WorkScreen.tsx` rather than a `use-work.ts`, so no
  `WEB_NO_MIRROR_REQUIRED` entry was needed.
- Added a geometric assertion to the browser test after a 390px screenshot
  showed the space cell clipped while every text assertion passed; verified it
  goes red under the old layout.
