**Decisions**
- `lazy` on the Tooltip rather than a second component: a board draws a chip,
  an avatar and a marker per field per card; mounting a hidden tooltip and ten
  listeners for each, most never hovered, is the cost a `title` never had.
  The description joins `aria-describedby` only when the tooltip exists.
- Card and row markers get hover/long-press tooltips but **no new tab stop** —
  the card or row is the keyboard's stop and already says it.
- Disabled reasons by kind, not blanket: busy keeps native `disabled` and its
  spinner (LAI-293); self-evident edges stay; input- or state-dependent ones
  say why, focusably (`aria-disabled` + Tooltip) or by linking the visible
  line that already says it.
- `DisabledReason` keeps one Tooltip always mounted (empty words never show) so
  a button is not remounted, and does not lose focus, when its reason toggles.
- Shared primitives touched where they render in this area (Chip, Avatar,
  PersonLink, AgentChip, CopyButton); Dropdown's native trigger title and the
  profile's InfoTip left for their owners and listed.
