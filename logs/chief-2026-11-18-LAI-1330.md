**Decisions**

- **"Had them before" guard on the 404 rule.**
  - The plan's rule, a 404 on two runs an hour apart, would deactivate a guest, someone on
    another domain, or anyone whose Laika address is no one's in Entra.
  - So the first miss is recorded only for a row with an Entra id. The review also asks for
    one itself; a mutation proved that check is load-bearing for a row with none.
- **Leavers needs Photos and profile.** It is the read that finds leavers, enforced by a CHECK
  (`leavers_enabled <= profile_enabled`) and by `422 leavers_need_profile`. Turning it on also
  asks `user.deactivate`, as the hand action does.
- **A 403 or 400 on a photo removes nothing,** where a 404 does. A refusal says nothing about
  whether the person has a photo.
- **The breaker counts only the leavers it could actually take** (owners and the last admin
  are excluded first). Under ten members, every leaver is held, by the spec's 10% rule.
- **A store failure stops photos for the run, not the run.** Profiles are still read and kept.

**Discovered**

- **A person reactivated by hand while Microsoft still says they have left is deactivated
  again at the next run.** This is documented in OPERATIONS and D-161, and left for the owner
  to decide.
- **Capacity folds never-seen people into one line,** so a test about a person's card has to
  use someone with a status.
- **A card-fit test failed on 11 px text at 24 px.** Every converged avatar now uses 28 px or
  more wherever the 12 px floor applies.
