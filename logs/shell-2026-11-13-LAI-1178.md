**Decisions**
- The status stays *visible by name* on the chip (LAI-739's review), on line
  one beside the key. A dot alone would have dropped that ruling.
- Overdue means open and due before today in local time. It leaves the grid
  entirely, so an old day can never grow a column of chips again.
- "+N more" never reads "+1 more": a last chip fits where the button would go.
- On a phone the overdue lane offers no drag, since the agenda has no day to
  drop on.
- Toolbar filters come through one shared `toolbarFilter`. The Board's own
  inline reading is left alone rather than refactored into it.
