## 2026-09-18T19:45:00Z — [ops] lanes to the bottom, wider cards

**What changed**
- docs/DECISIONS.md — D-062: the lanes fill the page; the rail collapses to pay
  for wider cards.
- .tasks/backlog/LAI-475-lanes-fill-the-page-and-the-rail-collapses.md — SHELL.

**Decisions**
- **I measured the design before writing anything, and the measurement changed
  the task.** `docs/design/Laika 01 - Kanban Board.dc.html` against `shell`: the
  rail is `266px` in both, the lane strip is `1fr` in both. At the owner's
  ~2000px viewport our lanes are **~290px**; the same design at 1600px gives
  **~204px**. **The cards are wider than the design already.** Had I taken
  *"increase the task width"* as a fidelity defect I would have filed a task to
  fix a board that matched.
- **So the two halves are different kinds of thing, and the task says so.**
  Height is a defect; width is a preference beyond the design that has to be
  paid for. Kept them in one task because they are one owner request about one
  box, but under separate headings with separate criteria.
- **Asked the owner rather than picked.** Three ways to buy width — collapse the
  rail, move the stream below the board, or a wide lane minimum with horizontal
  scroll — and they are materially different products, not implementations.
  Owner chose the collapsible rail. **The third option would have changed
  nothing at the width they actually work at**, which is worth having said out
  loud before building it.
- **`calc(100dvh - 21rem)` is deleted, not corrected.** Made a criterion in those
  words, because "fix the number" is the obvious reading and it is wrong: the
  number has already been hand-tuned once (LAI-272, `19rem → 21rem`, to pay for
  `.board-main` padding) and would need tuning again on the next change above
  the board. **A corrected magic number is the same defect with a fresh date.**
- **Required the height be asserted at two viewport heights.** One height is
  exactly what a magic number already satisfies, so a single-height test cannot
  tell the fix from the bug.
- **Expanded stays the default and stays 266px.** A board that opens with its
  live stream hidden has removed a feature for anyone who never finds the
  toggle; and wanting room when the rail is shut says nothing about its width
  when open.
- **Told SHELL explicitly that the four small fidelity gaps are not the answer.**
  Our `minmax(12.875rem, 1fr)`, the 1px border, the 12/10px padding and the 12px
  gap differ from the design and together are worth ~4px of card width. That is
  a tempting thing to do and call it done.

**Discovered**
- **`21rem` has been tuned by hand twice.** LAI-272's own notes say the lane
  height "widens from `100dvh - 19rem` to `21rem` to pay for" the new
  `.board-main` padding. Two hand-derivations of one constant is the signal it
  should not be a constant — and it is *in review right now*, correct for what
  it was asked and about to be superseded by this.
- **The design has no lane height at all** — `align-items:flex-start`, content
  height. **So "like in design" cannot be taken from the file literally.** The
  owner's want is clear and is what to build; recorded in D-062 so nobody later
  "restores fidelity" by putting the lanes back to content height.
- **Our rail is `16.625rem` = exactly the design's `266px`.** I expected the
  rail to be the drift and it is pixel-exact. Worth recording because it is the
  measurement that killed the obvious theory.
- **`align-items: stretch` on `.kanban` contradicts the design's `flex-start`
  deliberately** (LAI-270 — the ragged row the owner reported). Flagged in the
  task as a thing not to quietly revert while making the lanes fill.
