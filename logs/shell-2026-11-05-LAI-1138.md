**Decisions**

- ~~Drafts are always read (`drafts=include`) and *Confirmed only* counts them
  out on the client.~~ **Wrong, corrected in review round 1 (below):** that
  read other people's drafts unasked, against D-088 items 2–3 and SPEC
  §3.1/§6.4. Only the reader's own report (`me`) reads drafts unasked; every
  other scope asks only when *Include drafts* is turned on.
- Space, Member and Kind filter on the server, because `by_day` carries no
  row detail — the bars could not follow a client-side filter. Filter options
  are every space and person seen for this reader, so narrowing does not empty
  its own menu.
- A space admin (D-136, D-138) gets Week · Month · Range (no Day), the stretch
  widened to whole ISO weeks and stopped at this week; one space chip; a
  weekly bar list with *withheld — fewer than 3 others*; no table, donut,
  drafts switch, Member or Kind.
- A personal report never sends an unnamed query (it would be everyone's):
  My day keeps Day until the reader's id is known.
- Durations in the table are `h:mm`, not the design's `h:mm:ss`: the server
  sends minutes. The strip prints the design's short figures on a phone.
- *Time this week* on the profile Overview is a link: reading figures there
  would log a look at the person on every Overview open (D-102, D-135).
