## 2026-11-13T14:25:01+05:30 — LAI-1179 (server half, CHIEF)

**Why.** Owner: switching Day/Week/Month/Year on /my-day lags against the design
(*My Day Calendar Zoom (standalone)*). Measured causes, in order: the client
waits for the data before it animates (the design starts on the click frame),
the outgoing layer is rebuilt when its role changes, the stage collapses to a
loading card, re-renders during the 520 ms, a dead z-index rule, Day not a
zoom target via the switch, a race on fast switches — all web (the builder's
half). On the server, `daysOf` read every day with two queries.

**Measured** (tsx script over a copy of the pre-cutover prod database,
Pawan's 536 entries, this laptop): before — month 10.5 ms, 92 days 38.6 ms, a
year's four chunks 108.7 ms; after — 1.6 / 4.5 / 6.4 ms. Week's seven
`entriesOn` reads were 1 ms, so the server is not where Week's wait comes from.

**What changed.** `server/src/services/time-entries.ts` `entriesBetween`,
`server/src/services/time-drafts.ts` `draftsBetween` (the range twins of
`entriesOn`/`draftsOn`, same selects, `between(day, from, to)`, oldest day
first); `server/src/services/time-days.ts` buckets the two reads by day.
Output unchanged (the four existing cases hold); a new case pins two
statements for three days and for ninety-two, the day before `from` and after
`to` out, a deleted entry and a dismissed draft counting for nothing.

**Mutation.** Dropping `isNull(deletedAt)` from `entriesBetween` → the new case
red (deleted 15 min counted); restored byte-identical. A first mutant that
widened the upper bound stayed green — over-fetching outside the stretch cannot
change a result bucketed by day — so the two-statement count is the guard for
the range, not a boundary assertion.

eslint/prettier 0.

**Decisions**
- Slides and switches arriving while busy are coalesced (latest kept, played at `finish`), not ignored as the design's `jump()` does — the address has already changed on the click, so ignoring would leave the stage disagreeing with the header. Same choreography, one source of truth.
- The Day layer is one layer whatever its day (`keyOf` = `day`): Day's own ‹ › change its content, not its layer, so the Timeline is never remounted on a day step and Day stays the page LAI-1174 left it. The design slides Day too; not done here.
- The stage's held height is released at `finish` (the brief's choice): a Year → Week zoom ends with the page shortening by the difference. An eased release was not attempted — it would be layout work every frame and the scroll position clamps anyway.
- The `seq` token is kept although the queue makes it redundant for concurrent zooms; the queue is what the rapid-switch test guards, and the task file says so rather than claiming the token is covered.
- Prefetch after a Day read runs on `[dayReady, selectedDay, subjectId]`, not on `tick`: a change on Day empties the cache, and re-reading a year after every edit would be the cost the cache is there to avoid. The first zoom out after an edit lands on the placeholder and fills.
- Foreground Year reads its four stretches in parallel (one RTT); the background reads them one at a time — the brief's HTTP/1.1 budget argument applies to what competes with the page, not to what the reader is waiting for.
