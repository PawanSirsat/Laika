1Laika · screen 1 — Kanban board

Same family as the reference board: light page, tinted lane tubs, white cards, Plus Jakarta Sans + JetBrains Mono for keys and counters. Three things are new and specific to Laika — a priority dot leading each card footer, a bot badge clipped to the assignee avatar when an agent did the last write, and a live layer that actually moves: the event stream in the rail advances on its own, and whichever card just changed carries a fading ring plus a "just now" stamp. 1a is live — toggle theme, filter to agent work, filter by person, watch a card update. 1b is the card anatomy, 1c the dark pair and the empty lane.
